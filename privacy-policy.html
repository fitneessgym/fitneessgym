Privacy Policy

Fitness Gym · Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fitness Gym ("Fitness Gym", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses information when you use our website and mobile application (collectively, the "Services").

Important: This policy is intended for the Fitness Gym website and mobile application. It should accurately match the data and features actually used by the published app, including any third-party services or SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, username, and authentication information.
  • Gym and membership information: membership status, subscription details, membership dates, and related account records.
  • Fitness information you choose to provide: workout plans, completed exercises, repetitions, sets, weights, goals, progress records, and similar training information.
  • Orders and products: information needed to process gym product orders or requests, such as selected products, quantities, delivery/contact details, and order history.
  • Communications: information you provide when contacting the gym or submitting a support request.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system, app version, browser information, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, and diagnostic or usage information where technically necessary.

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information such as precise GPS location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone recordings, camera content, or health/fitness data from device health platforms unless a specific feature is introduced and the required disclosure and permissions are provided first.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide membership and gym services.
  • Display your workout plans, workout history, progress, and other features you request.
  • Process and manage product orders or service requests.
  • Respond to support and customer-service requests.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate the Services, such as with service providers that host data, provide authentication, analytics, communications, payment processing, or other technical services.

Service providers are expected to use information only for the services they provide to us and to protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Fitness Gym, our users, or others.

5. Payments

If the Services support payments, payment card information should be processed by the applicable payment provider rather than stored directly by Fitness Gym unless explicitly stated otherwise. Payment providers may process payment information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Fitness Gym account and associated personal information by contacting Fitness Gym through the contact/support method available on our website or inside the application. We will process eligible deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to information that must be retained to meet legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting requirements.

Deleting an account may permanently remove access to membership records, workout history, progress records, orders, and other account-related content.

9. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for authentication, security, preferences, analytics, or website functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

The Services may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Fitness Gym is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that we do not control.

12. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, update, delete, or restrict the processing of certain personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing or request a copy of information we hold about you.

To exercise an applicable right, contact Fitness Gym through the support/contact method provided on the website or in the application.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Services, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after an update means the updated policy will apply to the extent permitted by law.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy requests, or account/data deletion, please contact Fitness Gym through the Contact Us / Support method provided on our website or inside the application.
